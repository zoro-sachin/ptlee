About Us

A Legacy of Excellence in Engineering Education

Founded in 1994, PT Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker College of Engineering & Technology stands as a beacon of technical excellence in Tamil Nadu. Affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE, we nurture future engineers with cutting-edge curriculum, world-class facilities, and industry-driven pedagogy.

Our commitment extends beyond academics — we shape leaders, innovators, and nation-builders who make a lasting difference in society and industry.
